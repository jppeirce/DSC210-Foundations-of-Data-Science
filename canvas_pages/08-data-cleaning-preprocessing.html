🧹 Data Cleaning and Preprocessing

Modules 7 and 8 live inside the same box. In Module 7 we explored the taxi data and produced an inventory of problems. Now we act on that inventory, and the acting will send us back to explore more than once. By the end we will have a documented, analysis-ready table and be able to defend every row we changed.

Garbage in, garbage out. No model rescues a dataset nobody cleaned.

💡 Key concepts:
  • Explain why cleaning is a series of documented judgment calls rather than a default setting
  • Detect missing values, including disguised ones, and judge whether the missingness is systematic
  • Choose between deletion and imputation, and defend the trade-off
  • Repair wrongly typed or inconsistently formatted columns, and remove duplicate records
  • Combine tables with pd.merge(), choosing the join type that shows the absences you need to see
  • Assemble a reproducible cleaning script and write the data notes that make it safe to hand on
