Module 13 · Capstone

🏠 What Makes a House Sell for More?

A regional assessor's office has five years of residential sales records and one question: what actually drives what a house sells for, and how accurately can we predict a sale price before it happens? You will take that from a vague sentence to a defended answer using the full arc of the course. Nothing here is a new technique.

2,930 sales in Ames, Iowa, 2006 to 2010. Big enough and messy enough to be worth doing properly.

💡 Key concepts:
  • Work the full loop: ASK → GET → EXPLORE → MODEL → COMMUNICATE, on one dataset
  • Audit feature types and missing data on a table with dozens of columns
  • Explore one variable at a time, then explore relationships between them
  • Look for structure without the label, then predict price as a regression
  • Reframe the same question as a classification, and say what that reframing costs
  • Assemble the findings into a written memo an assessor's office could act on
