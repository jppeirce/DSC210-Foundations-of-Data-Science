Before you start: a quick review

Everything on this assignment comes from the Module 1 and Module 2 notebooks. If any line below is unfamiliar, reread that section first.

From Module 1

  • Our working definition: data science is the art of gaining, interpreting, and communicating insights from complex data through digital techniques.
  • The three circles: math and statistics, hacking skills, and substantive expertise. Missing one changes what a project can do, and what it can get wrong.
  • The 5-C checklist for the ethical dimensions of a project.

From Module 2

  • Cases, features, and metadata: the vocabulary for describing any dataset.
  • Structured vs. unstructured data, and why metadata matters to anyone analyzing the structured part.
  • Feature types: nominal, ordinal, interval, ratio, and which summaries each one permits. The key question is not what Python will compute, but what is meaningful.
  • What makes data big (the V's), and where data comes from. A dataset is never just "found."
  • The workflow: ASK → GET → EXPLORE → MODEL → COMMUNICATE, with findings looping back to new questions.
