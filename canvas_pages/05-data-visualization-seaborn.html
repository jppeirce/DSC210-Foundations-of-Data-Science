📊 Data Visualization with seaborn

Summary statistics alone can lie to you; two datasets with identical means and correlations can look wildly different. That is why data scientists always plot. Today we meet seaborn, which builds on matplotlib to produce statistical plots from tidy data in one line, and we learn to read plots as critically as we make them.

💡 Key concepts:
  • Explain why summary statistics alone can mislead, and why we always plot
  • Build the three workhorse plots with seaborn: histogram, boxplot, scatterplot
  • Add a categorical dimension with hue, and label a figure so it stands on its own
  • Read a plot critically: shape, spread, outliers, relationships, and dishonest axes
  • Choose an appropriate plot for a question
